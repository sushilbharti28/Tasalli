## Short description (80 characters max)
Phone call reminders for your parents' medicines, in their own language.

## Full description

Your parents may not use a smartphone. They do have a phone. Tasalli calls them at medicine time, in their own language, and tells you whether they said they took it.

**How it works**
1. Add your parent: name, phone number and the language they like (Hindi, English, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil or Telugu).
2. We call them once to ask if they agree to reminder calls. Nothing is scheduled before they say yes.
3. Add each medicine the way your family says it: "the white round BP tablet", one tablet, after breakfast. You enter every detail. Tasalli repeats it exactly.
4. At medicine time your parent gets a call. They say "le li" or "haan", or press 1.
5. You see on your phone, in the app, what they said. If a call is not answered, we try again and then tell you.

**What you get**
- Calls on any phone, including a basic keypad phone. Your parent needs no app and no internet.
- Calls in six languages, in short plain sentences.
- Your own words for each medicine, and how it looks, so your parent knows which one is meant.
- Today and history screens, "mark as taken" if you confirm another way, and a test call to your own phone.
- Alerts by notification and WhatsApp when a dose is missed, and one summary in the evening.
- Your parent can press 9 at any time to stop all calls, and we will never call that number again.

**Plans**
7-day free trial for one person. Then Rs 299 a month for one parent, Rs 499 a month for two parents, or Rs 4,999 a year for the family. Paid by UPI autopay through Razorpay. Cancel any time.

**Your data**
Stays in India. You can ask us to delete it at any time from the app or by writing to support. See the privacy policy.

**Important**
Tasalli is a reminder service. It is not medical advice and does not suggest, check or change any medicine or dose. It only repeats what your family entered. Please ask a doctor or pharmacist about any medicine.
