# Family onboarding guide (DRAFT, needs native-speaker review before translation)

Send this to a family after sign-up. Written to be forwarded on WhatsApp. Short sentences on purpose.

## Before you start (2 minutes)
- Keep the medicine strips or bottles in front of you.
- Tell your parent that a call from Tasalli is coming. A heads-up helps them pick up.
- Check that your parent's phone can get calls and has network.

## Step 1. Sign in
Open the app. Enter your mobile number. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS.

## Step 2. Add your parent
Tap "Add a person", then "A parent or relative". Enter their nickname (what you call them), full name, phone number, and the language for calls. Say whether they can read the name printed on a strip.

## Step 3. The first call (consent)
We call your parent and say your name. They say "haan" or press 1 to agree. If they press 9, we never call again and we tell you. Nothing is scheduled until they agree. If they do not pick up, we try two more times. You can also tell them to expect it.

If it is your own number, no consent call is needed.

## Step 4. Add each medicine
The app asks one thing at a time.
1. What is it: tablet, capsule, syrup or something else.
2. Name from the strip, strength as printed, and **what your parent calls it** ("BP wali", "sugar ki goli").
3. What it looks like: colour, shape, size, any line or letters.
4. Where it is kept: strip, loose, or bottle. Pack colour and print colour help.
5. How much, and before or after food, and the time.

Use only what the doctor's prescription says. Tasalli never chooses or changes an amount. It says exactly what you typed.

## Step 5. Look-alike check
If two medicines look similar, the app asks you to write a number on one strip, or to say the printed name on the call. This compares how they look, nothing else. Write the number with a marker. Every Sunday, we remind your parent to mark any new strip.

## Step 6. Test call
Tap "Test call" and Tasalli calls your own phone. Listen to how it sounds. Fix any wording in the medicine name.

## What your parent will hear
A call at medicine time. They say "le li" (or "haan") or press 1 to confirm. Press 2 to be reminded again in 15 minutes (once). Press 0 to hear it again. Press 3 if the doctor changed the medicine; we tell you. Press 9 to stop all calls.

## When the doctor changes a medicine
Open the app, edit that medicine, and check the call wording again. Do the test call.

## If a dose is missed
We call again after 10 minutes, twice. Then you get an alert in the app and on WhatsApp. Please call your parent yourself.

## Help
WhatsApp us from the app's Settings screen. Tasalli is a reminder service. It is not medical advice.
