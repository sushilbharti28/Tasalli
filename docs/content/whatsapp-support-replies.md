# WhatsApp support replies (DRAFT, needs founder review)

Short, warm, plain. Fill {name}. Never give medical advice or comment on a dose, a medicine or a symptom. For anything about health, use reply 14.

1. **Greeting.** Namaste {name} ji, this is Tasalli support. How can we help?
2. **Parent did not get a call.** Sorry about that. Please check that {parent}'s phone has network and is not on Do Not Disturb, and that they have said yes to the consent call. Open the person's page in the app: it shows consent and the last call result. Send us a screenshot and we will look.
3. **Consent call not answered.** We try 3 times. If they missed all, tap "Call again" on the person's page. A message from you before the call helps them pick up.
4. **Parent pressed 9.** That means they do not want calls. We stopped all calls to that number and cannot restart them ourselves. If they changed their mind, please ask them directly, then add them again with a new consent call.
5. **Wrong name or wording on the call.** Open the medicine in the app, change what the parent calls it, and run "Test call". The call says exactly what you typed.
6. **Wrong time.** Times follow the parent's time zone. Open the person's page and check the time zone and the dose times.
7. **Call language.** You can change the call language on the person's page. Hindi and English are ready first. Bengali, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu scripts are still being checked with native speakers; tell us if anything sounds odd.
8. **Parent says "I did not take it" but the app shows taken.** Open Today, tap the dose, and mark it correctly. We are sorry for the confusion. Send us the time and we will check the call log.
9. **Mark as taken.** If you know they took it, tap the dose and "Mark as taken".
10. **Billing.** Plans are Rs 299 a month (one parent), Rs 499 a month (two parents), Rs 4,999 a year (family), after a 7-day trial. Paid by UPI autopay. You can cancel in Settings, Plans.
11. **Refund request.** Sorry it did not work for you. We have noted it and the founder will reply within one working day.
12. **Delete my data.** We can delete your account and everything entered. Please confirm by replying DELETE from the registered number. We will confirm when done.
13. **Stop all calls to my parent.** Open the person and tap Pause, or tell us and we will stop it now.
14. **Questions about a medicine, a dose or a health problem.** We are not able to help with that, because Tasalli only repeats what your family entered. Please ask the doctor or pharmacist. If it is an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
15. **Closing.** Thank you {name} ji. Message us any time.
