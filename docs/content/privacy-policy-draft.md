## 1. What Tasalli is
Tasalli sends automated reminder calls to people (usually parents) about medicines that a family member has entered. Tasalli repeats what the family entered. It is not medical advice.

## 2. Who is who
You, the app user, are a person who enters details. The person who receives calls is the "person called". Tasalli decides why and how the data is used (the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act).

## 3. Data we collect
- You: mobile number, name, sign-in code records, plan and payment status (payments handled by Razorpay; we do not store card or UPI details), device token for notifications.
- Person called: name, nickname, phone number, call language, time zone, consent record (time, how given).
- Medicines you enter: names, strengths, amounts, times, how they look, how they are kept. This is health-related information.
- Calls: time, duration, outcome, the key pressed or the words recognised ("haan", "nahi"). We do not keep call recordings. [Confirm with call-engine and providers.]
- Support chats you send us.

## 4. Why we use it (purposes)
To place reminder calls, show you results, send missed-dose alerts, run billing, give support, keep the service working, and meet legal duties. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 5. Consent
- **Person called:** no reminder call is scheduled until they agree on the consent call ("haan" or key 1). They can press 9 on any call, or say a stop phrase, and we will never call that number again, across all accounts. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
- **You:** by signing in and entering a person's details you confirm you have the right to do so and that the person called knows about it. [Lawyer: parental/guardian consent where a person called cannot consent; confirm wording.]
- Consent notices will be available in English and the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule where reasonable. [Lawyer to confirm timing.]

## 6. Who we share with
Service providers that act for us: telephony (Twilio, Exotel or Plivo), SMS (MSG91), WhatsApp Business, Firebase (notifications), Razorpay (payments), cloud hosting in India. They may only use data to serve us. We share with authorities only when the law requires it.

## 7. Where data lives
Health-related data is stored in India (Mumbai region). [Lawyer: confirm handling by telephony providers whose systems may process call metadata outside India.]

## 8. How long we keep it
While your account is active. After you delete a person or your account, we delete their data within [30] days, except records we must keep by law (for example billing records) and the block list (a phone number that pressed 9 is kept, as a number only, so we never call it again).

## 9. Your rights (DPDP Act)
You and the person called can ask us to: tell you what data we hold and why; correct or complete it; **delete it**; withdraw consent; and name someone to act for you. You can also complain to us and then to the Data Protection Board of India.
How: in the app (Settings, Delete my data), by WhatsApp or email to [grievance officer]. We reply within [7] days.

## 10. Security
Encryption in transit and at rest, limited staff access, phone numbers masked in logs. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Board as the law requires.

## 11. Children
The app is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data of children for calls.

## 12. Changes
We will tell you in the app before material changes.
